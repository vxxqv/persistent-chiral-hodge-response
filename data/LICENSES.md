# Data attribution and license map

- `conformers.json`: original generated ETKDGv3/MMFF94s geometries by Vivaan Patni, CC BY 4.0; source chemical identities are documented PubChem identifiers. No experimental-structure or thermodynamic sampling claim.
- `extension_sources/published_embeddings_selected.npz`, selected external coordinates in `results/external_full/parents`, and external derived result tables: EscheWang/3dcs, CC BY-SA 4.0. Dataset revision b877dd661469358e8fe0aa40f09ef87a75dec929; embedding revision 8d6548c090592ba747750e501096398608b464da. Source: https://huggingface.co/datasets/EscheWang/3dcs . Original source hashes and row indices are retained. No full source parquet or author notebooks are included. Attribution: Wang et al., 3DCS, ICLR 2026, https://openreview.net/forum?id=JAb0y8lkqL ; original embedding methods GemNet (2021), MACE (2022), FMG (2025).
- `extension_sources/3dcs`, `extension_sources/chiral_cliffs`, `biological_case_documents.json`, ChEMBL structural discovery records and `results/biology_*`: ChEMBL, EMBL-EBI, CC BY-SA 3.0. Source: https://www.ebi.ac.uk/chembl/ . Individual activity, assay, target, molecule and document identifiers are retained. Attribution: Zdrazil et al., Nucleic Acids Research 52(D1), D1180-D1192 (2024), DOI 10.1093/nar/gkad1004. The original scientific publications remain identified in each pair. No claim of ownership over these source measurements.
- Chiral Cliffs supplied chemical discovery identities only: Schneider et al. (2018), DOI 10.1002/cmdc.201700798. Fresh ChEMBL records supply the analyzed measurements. The publisher's supplementary PDF and author notebooks are excluded.
- `activity_discovery_cohort.json` records the zero-eligible-pair MoleculeACE census, source commit and file hashes; it contains no republished potency table. It is acquisition provenance, not an extra endpoint dataset.
- Original pilot/geometry results and original analytic figure-source values: Vivaan Patni, CC BY 4.0. Figure-source records containing external or ChEMBL-derived values retain the applicable source license above. Original code throughout `src`, `scripts`, `tests` and `figures`: MIT.
- `protein_sources/1J85.cif`, `2EFV.cif`, `1UBQ.cif` and their PDB entry metadata are distributed under the wwPDB CC0 1.0 public-domain dedication. Source: https://www.wwpdb.org/about/usage-policies . Retrieval URLs and SHA-256 hashes are in `protein_sources/retrieval_receipt.json`. Structure authors retain attribution: Lim et al. (1J85), DOI 10.1002/prot.10323; Thiruselvam et al. (2EFV), DOI 10.1016/j.bbrc.2016.11.052; Vijay-Kumar et al. (1UBQ), DOI 10.1016/0022-2836(87)90679-6. Their scientific articles are not redistributed.
- `results/constrained`, `results/quantum_kinematics` and `results/molecular_fields` derive from ChEMBL chemical identities and retain CC BY-SA 3.0 attribution. `results/protein_knots` contains original derived coordinate diagnostics from the CC0 PDB entries, CC BY 4.0. Exact abstract braid fixtures and original numerical validation records are CC BY 4.0. No manuscript, bibliography, research plan or proof notes are part of these datasets.
