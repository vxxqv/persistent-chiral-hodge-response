Date: 28 October 2026. Baseline: research plan dated 26 October 2026.

## Exact target

For a labelled configuration in R^3, construct a degree-two source on an oriented Rips complex K_r that is equivariant under signed simplex permutations and odd under improper Euclidean isometries. Use the standard unweighted persistent Laplacian for K_r included in K_s. Prove the response and correctly typed linear readout laws, fixed-chain-space perturbation and residual bounds, and limitations of scalar pooling. Execute finite checks on exact fixtures and generated molecular conformers.

## Routes

R1: prove symmetry by signed permutation conjugation. Discriminator: reverse vertex ordering and atom numbering. Failure means an orientation convention or cochain is wrong.

R2: construct the persistent up operator by an orthogonal null-space projector and independently by Schur complement. Discriminator: tetrahedral boundary killed at the later scale, plus nullities independently computed by exact homology rank. Failure means the persistent object is wrong.

R3: seek obstructions to exaggerated utility. Discriminators: complete-simplex collapse, reflection-symmetric fixtures, pooled cancellation, raw-source comparisons, and conformer variation. Negative outcomes remain results.

## Boundaries

The construction is not asserted to be a complete invariant. Exact mirror separation is equivalent to a nonzero odd feature, not to a biological outcome. R/S labels are not signed optical rotations. No affinity, pharmacological activity, or superiority claim follows from these experiments. Fixed-complex stability does not imply global continuity across filtration events.

Review provenance is self-review unless an independent reviewer is explicitly recorded. No numerical run certifies absence of all errors or global novelty.
